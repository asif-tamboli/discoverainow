Buyer guide

Choose a research tool by where your evidence lives.

The “best” research tool changes depending on whether you need the open web, your own documents, or a general-purpose reasoning partner.

Open webPerplexity

Good fit for cited web discovery and fast source collection.

What to test before choosing

NeedTest
Source qualityCan you open and verify the source behind an important claim?
FreshnessDoes the result reflect the date range you actually need?
GroundingCan it distinguish supplied evidence from its own prior knowledge?
Long sourcesCan it preserve details across several documents without flattening disagreements?
ExportabilityCan you move notes, sources and citations into the final brief?
Recommendation ruleIf the answer matters enough to act on, the source should be easy to inspect. Convenience is not a substitute for traceability.

Source-grounded research

Google describes NotebookLM as grounded in user-provided sources with citations; Perplexity emphasizes cited web research. Both can still be inaccurate, so verify consequential claims.
