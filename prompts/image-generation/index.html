Image prompt gallery

Image prompts with structure you can actually reuse.

These templates separate subject, composition, lighting, lens/style and exclusions so you can change the idea without rebuilding the entire prompt.

Photorealistic portrait

Editorial portrait with natural depth

Best for: photorealismVariables: subject, setting, wardrobeAspect: portrait
Create a photorealistic editorial portrait of [SUBJECT] in [SETTING]. Use soft directional window light, realistic skin texture, natural color separation, shallow depth of field, and subtle environmental context. Camera feel: full-frame photography, 85mm portrait lens, eye-level framing. Wardrobe: [WARDROBE]. Mood: [MOOD]. Keep facial proportions natural and avoid plastic skin, exaggerated sharpening, distorted hands, text, logos, or artificial beauty-filter effects.
Why this prompt is structured this way

It specifies lighting, lens feel and exclusions separately from the subject, which makes the visual treatment reusable across different people and settings.

Product photography

Premium studio product shot

Best for: ecommerce / adsVariables: product, surface, accent
Create a premium studio product photograph of [PRODUCT]. Place it on [SURFACE] with a clean background in [BACKGROUND TONE]. Use one soft key light from the upper-left, controlled rim lighting, realistic contact shadows, accurate materials, and crisp but natural detail. Composition should leave negative space on [LEFT/RIGHT] for optional copy. Accent prop: [OPTIONAL PROP]. No text, watermark, duplicate products, warped packaging, impossible reflections, or floating objects.
Common failure mode

Image models often distort packaging, labels and reflective surfaces. Treat this as a concept-generation prompt unless exact product fidelity has been verified.

Cinematic scene

Story-driven cinematic frame

Best for: posters / conceptsVariables: character, location, time
Create a cinematic still of [CHARACTER OR SUBJECT] in [LOCATION] during [TIME / WEATHER]. Frame the scene as a [WIDE / MEDIUM / CLOSE] shot with a clear foreground, middle ground and background. Lighting should come naturally from [LIGHT SOURCE]. Use restrained cinematic contrast, atmospheric depth, believable scale and realistic environmental texture. Emotional tone: [TONE]. Avoid over-saturated teal/orange grading, excessive fog, floating particles, unreadable signage and impossible anatomy.
Phone wallpaper

Minimal premium vertical wallpaper

Best for: lock screensAspect: tall vertical
Create a premium vertical smartphone wallpaper featuring [SUBJECT]. Keep the upper 25% visually calm for lock-screen clock readability. Position the main subject below center with strong depth, realistic materials and subtle atmospheric lighting. Use a clean edge-to-edge composition with no text, logos, borders or watermarks. Preserve important details away from the very top, bottom and side edges. Style: [PHOTOREALISTIC / MINIMAL / CINEMATIC / ILLUSTRATIVE].

How to adapt

Change the bracketed variables first. Only change camera/style language when the visual result needs a different treatment.
