Marketing prompts

Marketing prompts that start with the customer, not adjectives.

Use AI to sharpen positioning and structure. Do not let it invent customer proof, market numbers or product capabilities.

Positioning

Clarify why someone should care

Using only the product and customer information below, create a positioning draft. Return: - target user, - painful job/problem, - current alternative, - unique capability, - proof we actually have, - one-sentence positioning, - 3 message angles. Do not invent customer quotes, market statistics, certifications or capabilities. INPUT: [PRODUCT + CUSTOMER EVIDENCE]
Landing page

Build a conversion-focused outline

Create a landing-page outline for [PRODUCT] aimed at [AUDIENCE]. Goal: [ACTION]. Use this order: 1. problem-aware headline, 2. concise value proposition, 3. how it works, 4. proof, 5. use cases, 6. objections / FAQ, 7. CTA. For every proof statement, mark whether evidence was supplied. Do not invent testimonials or numbers.
SEO content brief

Write for intent, not keyword stuffing

Create a content brief for the query [QUERY]. Identify: - likely search intent, - questions the user needs answered, - information that would make the page genuinely better than generic results, - evidence / examples needed, - useful comparison tables or diagrams, - internal links, - CTA that naturally follows the user's intent. Do not propose filler sections just to make the article longer.

Best use

Use AI to structure and challenge messaging. Keep real customer evidence, proof and claims under human control.
