Workflow

Turn meeting notes into actions without inventing owners or deadlines.

AI is very good at cleaning up notes—and very capable of quietly turning suggestions into commitments. This workflow prevents that.

01Capture the raw record

Transcript, notes or recording summary. Keep the source unchanged.

02Extract facts only

Decisions, questions, actions, named owners and explicit deadlines.

03Mark missing fields

Use “Unassigned” and “Not specified” instead of guessing.

04Separate discussion from decision

Ideas raised are not automatically decisions made.

05Send a confirmation summary

Give attendees a compact list of decisions and actions to correct.

Copyable prompt

Convert these meeting notes into: DECISIONS - only decisions explicitly made ACTION ITEMS - task - owner (only if explicitly named) - due date (only if explicitly stated) - dependency OPEN QUESTIONS - unresolved items RISKS / BLOCKERS - only those actually discussed Do not guess owners, deadlines or decisions. Use "Unassigned" and "Not specified" where needed. NOTES: [PASTE NOTES]
