Tool guide

NotebookLM is a strong fit when the answer should stay grounded in your sources.

Instead of searching the whole web, give it the documents you care about and use citations to move back to the source.

Editorially reviewed
Guide updated: Sep 2026 · Editorial fit and workflow guidance. Check the vendor’s official site for current pricing, plan limits and newly released features.
Best forPDFs, research papers, notes, study material, project documents and source-grounded synthesis.
Watch forMissing source context, outdated uploaded material and summaries that flatten disagreements.

Useful sequence

  1. Upload a focused source set.
  2. Ask for claims and citations before synthesis.
  3. Compare contradictory passages.
  4. Create a brief or study guide only after checking the source excerpts.
