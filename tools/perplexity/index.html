Tool guide

Perplexity is most useful when the web is your evidence base.

Use it to discover sources and get a cited starting point. Do not confuse citations with automatic correctness.

Editorially reviewed
Best forCurrent web research, source discovery, fast topic orientation and cited exploration.
Not enough forConsequential claims you have not opened and verified in the underlying sources.

A good workflow

  1. Ask a narrow research question with date/scope constraints.
  2. Open the strongest primary sources.
  3. Compare conflicts and definitions.
  4. Move verified findings into a sourced brief.

Alternative
