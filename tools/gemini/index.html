Tool guide

Gemini is most compelling when your work already lives in the Google ecosystem or spans multiple media types.

Use it when documents, images, files and Google-connected workflows need to come together. As with any general assistant, verify consequential factual claims and avoid assuming integration automatically means accuracy.

Editorially reviewed
Best forMultimodal analysis, Google-centered productivity, general reasoning, drafting and file-based work.
Watch forMixing model-generated interpretation with source facts, unclear permissions in connected workflows, and over-reliance on convenience.

Useful workflows

01Files → structured summary

Ask for claims, open questions and source-backed takeaways rather than a generic summary.

02Media → analysis

Define what to inspect and what counts as evidence before asking for conclusions.

03Draft → action

Use clear constraints when turning source material into emails, plans or presentations.
