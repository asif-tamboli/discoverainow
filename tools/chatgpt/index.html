Tool guide

ChatGPT is a strong generalist when the task needs conversation, iteration and mixed formats.

Use it when you want to move between writing, analysis, coding, images and structured problem-solving in one place. Treat important factual claims and business decisions as things to verify, not accept automatically.

Editorially reviewed
Guide updated: Sep 2026 · Editorial fit and workflow guidance. Check the vendor’s official site for current pricing, plan limits and newly released features.
Best forGeneral-purpose reasoning, writing, coding help, structured analysis, brainstorming, file-based work and iterative image creation.
Watch forConfident unsupported claims, vague prompts that produce generic output, and tasks where fresh or source-grounded evidence matters.

Useful workflows

01Research → brief

Use it after collecting reliable sources to compare claims and draft a decision-ready brief.

02Writing → revision

Give audience, intent, constraints and source material before asking it to rewrite.

03Code → verification

Ask for diagnosis, tests and proof of behavior instead of accepting a code patch on confidence alone.

04Image → iteration

Use conversational refinement to adjust composition, details and constraints step by step.
