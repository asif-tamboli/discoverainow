AI image workflow

AI product photography that survives the details.

Create a usable product-image workflow instead of repeatedly asking an image model to “make it premium.”

Where this job fails

Product geometry, packaging text, reflections and shadows are where attractive generations often stop being usable.

Premium studio product image prompt

Create a premium studio product photograph of [PRODUCT]. Preserve exact product geometry, packaging layout and material appearance. Place it on [SURFACE] against [BACKGROUND]. Use one soft key light, controlled rim light, realistic contact shadows and physically believable reflections. Leave clean negative space on [SIDE] for optional copy. Do not invent labels, logos, accessories or product features. After generation, compare the result against the reference for shape, text, materials and reflections.

Use a decision loop, not prompt roulette

1 · DefineFreeze the requirement

Write down what may change and what must remain exact.

2 · ChooseMatch the tool to the job

Use Prompt Lab to compare editorial fit and tradeoffs.

3 · GenerateRun a controlled prompt

Change one important variable at a time so revisions remain interpretable.

4 · VerifyInspect the misses

Check fidelity, text, anatomy, materials and constraints before publishing.
