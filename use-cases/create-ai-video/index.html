Do this with AI

Create an AI video by separating story, visuals and motion.

Do not start with “make a 30-second video.” Build a short shot plan first, then generate only the assets and motion you actually need.

Best-suited tools

RunwayStrong fit for generative video, image-to-video and editing workflows.Adobe FireflyUseful when AI video belongs inside a broader Adobe creative workflow.ChatGPTUseful for concept, shot planning, prompt iteration and supporting image creation.MidjourneyUseful for creating strong visual keyframes before animating them.

Workflow

01Write the objective

Platform, audience, product/message, duration and one action you want the viewer to take.

02Create a shot list

Describe 4–8 shots with subject, camera, motion and duration.

03Generate keyframes

Create consistent still images for important scenes before asking for motion.

04Add motion

Use concise motion prompts: camera move, subject action, environmental motion and timing.

05Edit and verify

Check continuity, product fidelity, text, timing, audio rights and CTA clarity.

Shot prompt template

SHOT [NUMBER] — [DURATION] SUBJECT: [WHO / WHAT] SCENE: [ENVIRONMENT] CAMERA: [SHOT SIZE + CAMERA MOVEMENT] ACTION: [ONE CLEAR SUBJECT ACTION] ENVIRONMENTAL MOTION: [WIND, LIGHT, PARTICLES, BACKGROUND MOVEMENT] LOOK: [LIGHTING, COLOR, MOOD] CONSTRAINTS: - preserve product/character identity, - no unrequested text or logos, - believable motion and anatomy, - avoid abrupt camera jumps, - keep continuity with the previous shot.
Verification checklistWatch once with sound off for continuity and composition, once focusing only on product/character consistency, and once focusing only on timing, text and CTA.
