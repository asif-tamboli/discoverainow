AI image workflow

AI posters where the words matter as much as the image.

Text-heavy graphics combine two difficult jobs: visual composition and exact typography. Treat the wording as a requirement, not decoration.

Where this job fails

Check every visible word, number, date, logo and hierarchy before publishing; attractive typography can still contain incorrect text.

Text-heavy poster prompt

Create a [POSTER TYPE] for [PURPOSE]. Required visible text, exactly as written: “[TEXT]”. Visual hierarchy: [HEADLINE] first, [SECONDARY] second, [CTA/DETAIL] third. Style: [STYLE]. Composition: [LAYOUT]. Keep typography legible at the intended output size. Do not add, remove, paraphrase or duplicate words. If exact text cannot be rendered reliably, leave a clean text-safe area for final typography in a design editor.

Use a decision loop, not prompt roulette

1 · DefineFreeze the requirement

Write down what may change and what must remain exact.

2 · ChooseMatch the tool to the job

Use Prompt Lab to compare editorial fit and tradeoffs.

3 · GenerateRun a controlled prompt

Change one important variable at a time so revisions remain interpretable.

4 · VerifyInspect the misses

Check fidelity, text, anatomy, materials and constraints before publishing.
