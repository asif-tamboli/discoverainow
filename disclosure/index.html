Trust & monetization

We intend to make money without selling the result.

DiscoverAINow may earn revenue from affiliate links, sponsorships, vendor referrals and advertising. Commercial relationships will be disclosed and kept separate from benchmark scoring.

Affiliate links

If we earn a commission when a reader purchases or signs up through a link, the relationship will be disclosed near the relevant recommendation. A commission does not increase a tool's benchmark score.

Sponsored placements

Sponsored listings and sponsored content will be labeled as sponsored. Vendors may pay for visibility, but not for an undisclosed favorable review or altered benchmark result.

Free products and access

If a vendor provides free access, credits or another material benefit for evaluation, we will disclose it on the relevant page.

Corrections

AI products change quickly. We will date material tests, show methodology, and correct factual errors when identified.

PrincipleRevenue should help us do more useful testing. It should not make the testing less trustworthy.

Questions?

Commercial relationships should be understandable before a reader clicks or buys. This page will evolve as monetization is activated.
